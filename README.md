## About the Novel

**Too Cold to Love: Letting My Wife and Child Go** is a completed emotional fantasy romance available to read online on **NovelsHub**.

A husband prepares a beautiful feast for his son's fifth birthday, believing it should be a special family celebration.

But behind the smiles lies a truth that changes everything.

His wife, Cheryl Bennett, and their son, Jasper Ford, have completed a mysterious quest that will allow them to leave this world and return to the place they truly belong.

They have already decided to abandon him.

What they don't realize is that he has finally reached the same conclusion.

He no longer wants to hold on to people who have already chosen to leave.

Instead of begging them to stay, he decides to let them go.

What follows is an emotional story about a broken marriage, family bonds, betrayal, loss, acceptance, and a man learning that sometimes letting go is the only way to begin living again.

## Main Characters

### Cheryl Bennett

Cheryl is the protagonist's wife, but she carries a secret connected to another world and a mysterious system.

Her completed quest gives her the opportunity to leave everything behind and return to her original world.

### Jasper Ford

Jasper is their young son.

His fifth birthday becomes the moment when the hidden truth about Cheryl and Jasper's plans finally comes to light.

### The Husband

Instead of desperately trying to stop his wife and child from leaving, he reaches a painful realization of his own.

If they have already chosen another life, he will no longer force himself to remain trapped in a family where he is no longer wanted.
